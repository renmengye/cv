\section{\sc Research Interests}
Areas: Machine learning, computer vision, meta learning, representation learning, few-shot learning, 
brain \& cognitively inspired learning, robot learning, self-driving vehicles

My key research question is: how do we enable human-like, agent-based machine
intelligence to continually learn, adapt, and reason in naturalistic
environments? Towards this goal of building a more general and flexible AI, my
research has centered on developing \textit{representation learning} and
\textit{meta-learning} algorithms.
